Medium A Prompt:Create an artwork that uses mixed-media painting and emphasizes curved lines, shapes, color, texture, and emphasis. Use acrylic paint, colored pencils, and brushes with layering techniques characteristic of this medium. The composition should feature a woman’s face with simple flowers growing around and across it.

Medium B Prompt:Create an artwork that uses relief printmaking and emphasizes curved lines, shapes, color, texture, and emphasis. Use a foam printing plate, printing ink, a brayer, and paper with relief printing techniques. The composition should feature the same woman’s face with simple flowers growing around and across it.

Reflection:I chose mixed-media painting for my first medium because it was something I wasn’t really familiar with, and I wanted to try something different. I thought it would be interesting to use different materials together and see how my artwork would turn out. When I switched to printmaking, I noticed that the texture and overall appearance changed. The painting had more detail and looked more colorful, while the printmaking version looked simpler. I feel like the painting showed more of the flowers and the face, while printmaking focused more on the shapes and lines. One thing I found difficult about printmaking was that it was harder to add small details. However, I also liked that it could make the same design more than once. The tools made a big difference too because with painting I could use brushes and different materials, but with printmaking I had to use ink and a printing plate. I think painting gave me more freedom to add things, while printmaking made me focus more on the design itself. Overall, I enjoyed trying both mediums because I got to see how the same artwork could look different just by changing the materials and process. My opinion of the artwork stayed mostly the same, but I liked seeing how much the medium could change its appearance.
